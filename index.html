All kinds of songs and memories for the future…

A wondrous collection of French chansons in all shapes and sizes…
from Charles Aznavour to Charles Trénet.

Rob Schouten in Trouw, 2018 ↗ · translated from Dutch

I have never been a lover of French chanson.

I have never been a lover, let alone a connoisseur, of French chanson: it was too poetic and too sweet for me, and told me too little about the time I lived in. There, I’ve said it…

…The lyrics of those songs also seemed to be about leaving: ‘Ne me quitte pas’, ‘Je ne regrette rien’…

…Last weekend, at the birthday party of a dear friend, an old-fashioned chansonnier appeared out of nowhere at the end and announced in charming Dutch that he wanted to sing a few songs for the birthday girl and for us. He plucked his guitar and sang of Paris waking up and of a love that will hopefully never pass. For a moment it felt as if all of us at the party were sitting in a cocoon, far from the raging world, listening to sweet songs of long ago…
